# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, generated by a Node build script from the menu JSON; no framework, no bundler; Google Fonts are the only external dependency. Hosted on Vercel. The new menu ships to its own GitHub repository and its own Vercel project; the main site (mandi-center-menu.vercel.app) changes only when the owner says so.

## Users

- **Guest at the table (primary).** Scans the QR code on the table, reads the menu on a phone (360–430 px) in a dim, busy room, often in a group of 2–6 (families, friends, colleagues). Decides what to order and how many people a mandi tray should feed, then orders from the waiter.
- **Visitor before coming (secondary).** Opens the same page from Instagram, 2GIS or a link to see what is served, the prices, the address and the hours.
- **Waiter (tertiary).** Glances at a guest's phone to read back the order.
- Languages: Russian (primary) and Kazakh.

## Product Purpose

An online menu for Mandi Center, a restaurant of Arabic (Yemeni) cuisine in Astana. Its main job is to let a guest look through the menu comfortably and decide: what each dish is, which portion, what it costs. Success: the guest finds the mandi and the right tray size in seconds, reads prices without effort, and the page feels made for this restaurant rather than produced by a template or an AI.

## Positioning

Mandi Center calls itself "the only Mandi Center in Astana". Mandi (spiced rice with meat, served on a shared tray) is the signature dish, sold for 1, 2 or 5 persons; the per-person price falls as the tray grows. Karak tea and maghribi are the signature drinks. Halal kitchen, no pork and no alcohol.

## Operating Context

- Entry: QR code on the table (currently points to the old site mandicenter.netlify.app), the Instagram profile link, 2GIS.
- Ordering happens with the waiter. The menu does not take orders, payments or reservations.
- A quiet order list is kept: a small "+" on each item, a "Мой список" bar that appears once something is added, a sheet with −/+ and the total, and a full-screen "Показать официанту" view with quantity × name, portion/volume/cooking method, line sums and the total. It lives only in the guest's browser (localStorage) and is never sent anywhere; the page says so.
- Address: Астана, ул. Максута Нарикбаева, 5. Hours: daily 11:00–01:00. Phone +7 778 245 35 72, WhatsApp, Instagram @mandi_center.kz, 2GIS link (all in `menu_improved.json` → `meta.links`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content: 3 sections (Кухня, Напитки, Десерты), 19 categories, 126 priced positions, total of all prices 355 455 ₸. Single source of truth: `menu_improved.json`; price reference: `menu_data.json`; integrity check: `node check_prices.js <file.html>` must print OK.
- Mandi (5 cards): portion choice 1 / 2 / 5 persons inside one card, per-person price for 2 and 5, a saving mark only when `perPersonVs.pct >= 5`. "Манди с судаком" has a separate cooking choice (барбекю / фрай) stored as a note on the order line, not a new item.
- Drinks have volume/size variants (0,5 л / 1 л, S / M, на компанию) chosen inside the card.
- "Вафельная трубочка с варёной сгущёнкой" (key 3-0-7) is out of stock: no price, add button disabled.
- Every item and every variant carries `data-key` in the HTML.
- Prices, dish names, composition and portions never change. Price format "3 800 ₸" with non-breaking spaces.
- Kazakh: all interface text and menu content get a Kazakh version behind a language switch. The Kazakh text is prepared by us and must be checked by the restaurant before it is treated as final.
- Open product questions (weights, allergens, spiciness, some prices, descriptions for 46 cards) are listed in `questions.md`; until answered, the menu shows the neutral line "О составе блюд и аллергенах спросите официанта".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mandi Center. No logo file is on hand; the name is set in type.
- Facts allowed on the page: cuisine "Арабская (йеменская) кухня", "Халяльная кухня", the tagline "Единственный Манди Центр в Астане", address, hours, contacts.
- Owner rejected so far: the light beige delivery-app look ("Тёплый песок") and the dark brick-and-stained-glass look ("Сана"). The owner wants an Eastern mood that fits this restaurant, and explicitly does not want gradients, colored text on colored fields, or fonts that read as AI-default.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real dish photos in `images/` (taken from the restaurant's site). Most are 140×140 px, so large crops look soft; better photos must come from the restaurant.
- Menu text and prices: `menu_original.md` (verbatim) and `menu_improved.json` (cleaned names, all changes logged in `changes.md`).
- Research of ~30 QR and restaurant menus: `research/menu_research.md`.
- Absent and must not be invented: weights, ingredients beyond the given descriptions, allergens, calories, ratings, reviews, certificates, halal certificate numbers, awards, delivery, online payment.

## Product Principles

1. The menu is for reading and choosing. Browsing comes first; the order list stays secondary and quiet.
2. Mandi leads. The signature dish and its tray sizes are found first and understood without explanation.
3. Truth over persuasion: only real prices, real names, real photos; nothing invented to sell harder.
4. One hand, one phone, a busy table: big targets, readable prices, nothing that needs precision or patience.
5. Belongs to this restaurant: specific to Mandi Center and its cuisine, never a generic template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Mobile first; no horizontal scroll at 360–430 px.
- Tap targets at least 44×44 px; text contrast at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); visible keyboard focus.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns animations off.
- Russian and Kazakh, both in Cyrillic; fonts must cover Kazakh letters (Ә Ғ Қ Ң Ө Ұ Ү Һ І) and the ₸ sign.
